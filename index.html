2026 · RAG application

ReviewLens: Evidence-backed answers from customer reviews

ReviewLens turns thousands of customer reviews into quick answers. Ask "What do customers complain about regarding coffee?" and get a short summary grouped into themes, with approximate counts and the IDs of the reviews behind each point.

It runs on 5,000 Amazon Fine Food Reviews, cleaned and sampled evenly across star ratings. A LangGraph workflow routes each question as a complaint, praise or general query, retrieves the most relevant reviews from Pinecone (filtering by star rating when it makes sense) and checks that they are relevant. Only then does Gemini write the answer. If nothing relevant turns up, it says so instead of guessing.

2026 · End-to-end ML app

RoomStyle AI: Interior Style Classifier & Paint Recommender

An end-to-end ML pipeline that classifies room photos into 4 design styles using colour-histogram features. Logistic Regression, Random Forest and a Neural Network were compared through cross-validation.

PCA and LDA were evaluated for dimensionality reduction, and the neural network architecture was tuned with GridSearchCV. It ships as a Streamlit app with live style prediction, confidence scores and paint colour recommendations.
